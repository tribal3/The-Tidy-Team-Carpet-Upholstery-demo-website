## 2. Sources consulted

1. **Google Maps listing** — `https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Tidy+Team+13+Bondfield+Road+Billesley+Birmingham`
   - Confirmed name, category, full address, phone, 4.7 rating and the absence of a website.
   - Camera/photo metadata: one listing photo credited to "Ben Smith", "Photo – Aug 2018", captured July 2018.
2. **Google Maps contributor profile (Ben Smith)** — `https://maps.google.com/maps/contrib/115556661149450439902/photos`
   - Public owner-uploaded photos: six images associated with The Tidy Team, one with an unrelated restaurant in Gibraltar (excluded).
3. **Cylex** — `https://birmingham.cylex-uk.co.uk/company/the-tidy-team-26859038.html`
   - Direct fetch returned HTTP 403. Content was read from the public Cylex category/search pages that syndicate the listing.
   - Review summary states customers praise "exceptional carpet cleaning services provided by Ben and his team", friendly staff, thorough cleaning, fresh results, honest/efficient/high-quality service and repeat recommendations, with "a few minor communication issues" noted.
4. **Yell** — `https://www.yell.com/biz/the-tidy-team-birmingham-9496601/`
   - Direct fetch returned HTTP 403. Public Yell search pages confirm the listing as "Carpet & Upholstery Cleaners" at 13 Bondfield Rd, B13 0JA, with no Yell ratings. The listed website destination could not be resolved.
5. **Client-supplied research** — 4.7 rating, 23 reviews, and three review snippets:
   - "Amazing quality service!"
   - "Can't thank him enough for his hard work and low price."
   - "Carpets came back much cleaner and give advice given."

**No stock photography was used.** No image was taken from another "Tidy Team" business. No before-and-after pairs were published because no genuine paired photographs could be verified — the two red-carpet shots are near-identical and their order cannot be established, so a comparison would have been fabricated. No team photo, business logo, or owner portrait was available; the site uses a typographic wordmark instead.

**Limitation to disclose to the client:** the photo set is small (five usable images) and was shot on a phone, largely in 2018. The gallery is therefore deliberately limited to genuine work rather than padded with irrelevant cleaning stock. High-quality project photography supplied by the business would materially improve the site.

## 6. Content rules applied

- British English throughout ("personalised", "enquiries", "colour").
- Rating and review count are presented as based on the Google Maps listing, with a link to that listing.
- Two review quotes are reproduced verbatim from the client-supplied research and labelled "Google review"; **reviewer names and dates are omitted rather than invented**. The third supplied snippet was not displayed verbatim because its wording could not be verified.
- The enquiry form is explicitly labelled as a demonstration and does not claim to deliver messages. The phone number is the working contact route.
- No opening hours, prices, guarantees, certifications, years of experience or service-area postcodes are claimed.
